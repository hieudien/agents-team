---
name: verify-real-artifact
description: Verify against the running product, not the dev's report.
---
1. Do not trust the dev's evidence; run the app or command yourself.
2. For each acceptance item, state what you did and what you observed, with the URL or command.
3. For a bug fix, confirm the original reproduction no longer fails and nearby behavior still works.
4. Report numbers (counts, timings) only after checking what they measured.
5. Anything you could not check goes in the checklist as not verified, never as pass.
